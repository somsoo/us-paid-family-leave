Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Paid Family Leave pay per week?

Depending on your state, paid family leave replaces between 60% and 90% of your average weekly wages, up to statutory state maximums ranging from $900 to over $1,600 per week.

How long can I take Paid Family Leave?

State-mandated paid family leave typically allows between 8 and 12 weeks of paid benefits per 12-month period for bonding with a new child or caring for an ill relative.

Is Paid Family Leave job-protected?

Most state paid leave statutes offer explicit job protection and health insurance maintenance. At the federal level, FMLA provides 12 weeks of unpaid job protection for qualifying workers.
